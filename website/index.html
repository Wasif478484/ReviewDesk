The problem

Unanswered reviews cost you customers.

Replying to everything with the same cheerful paragraph can make a bad situation worse. The value is in deciding whether a public reply should exist at all.

01

A health claim or legal threat answered with "We're sorry for any inconvenience" makes it worse.

02

A review naming a staff member needs facts verified before anything is posted.

03

A spam review is not a customer complaint. Reporting it is the right call, not replying.

04

The owner may not read the language the review is written in. They need a translation before posting.

The four decisions

Reply. Check facts. Hold. Report.

Every review gets exactly one. A genuine complaint is never labeled as a report, even when it's harsh or unfair.

Reply
Praise, mild feedback, ordinary complaints about service, food, price, wait, cleanliness.
Safe to reply
Check facts
Named staff, specific incident, unverifiable claim. Draft includes [OWNER: ...] placeholders.
Reply after verifying
Hold
Health claims, legal threats, harassment, safety incidents. No draft. Checklist only.
Do not reply yet
Report
Spam, ads, off-topic, abusive language, text not about this business.

Autopilot

Low-risk replies can post on their own.

Autopilot is optional, off by default, and starts in dry-run mode. Every automatic post must pass the safety checks in policy.py. Hold, report, and check-facts reviews never post automatically.

Veto window30 minutes by default. Each auto-post row shows a countdown. You can cancel before it goes out.
Trusted languagesEnglish only by default. Add a language only after a native reader has checked its output.
Daily capLimits autopilot posts only. Counts actual posts, not dry runs.
Dry runLogs what would be posted. Sends nothing.
Pause / UndoPause stops all posting. Undo calls delete on a posted reply.

Streamlit cannot run in the background when nobody has the page open. "Automatic" means the Run cycle button plus an optional auto-refresh while the page is open.

FAQ

Questions.

Does ReviewDesk post replies for me?

No. You copy the draft and paste it into your review platform by hand. Autopilot can post automatically, but only for low-risk reviews that pass all safety checks, and it starts off with dry run on.

What languages does it support?

Reviews can arrive in any language. Drafts are written in the reviewer's language, with a translation into the language you read. Urdu, Roman Urdu, Arabic, French, and Spanish have been checked by native readers. Other languages need verification before you claim support.

Is my review data safe?

There are no accounts and no database. Reviews live only in your browser session. Review text is sent to the model provider you choose (Anthropic or OpenAI). The README states this plainly.

Can I use it for multiple businesses?

Yes. Set up a profile per client in the sidebar. The app keeps no server-side state.

What about Google's own reply tools?

ReviewDesk's value is the triage: deciding whether to reply at all. Google's rules on automated replies have not been checked and should be reviewed before real use.
